Privacy

Privacy policy.

What we collect, how we use it, and how we protect it.

Effective November 23, 2026

Who we are

Golden Key Recovery LLC (“Golden Key,” “we,” “us”) runs an addiction treatment program in Troy, Missouri. This policy covers goldenkeyrecovery.com and the calls, texts and emails you exchange with us.

What we collect

We collect only what you choose to give us:

  • Your name, phone number and email address.
  • What you tell us about your situation or a loved one’s, such as who needs help, substances involved and how soon help is needed.
  • Voicemails, text messages and emails you send us.

This website does not use advertising trackers or sell advertising.

How we use it

  • To call or message you back about your inquiry.
  • To talk through admissions, scheduling and next steps.
  • To provide services if you or your loved one enrolls.
  • To meet our legal and professional obligations.

How we share it

We do not sell your information. We do not share it for anyone else’s marketing. We share it only:

  • With service providers that help us operate, such as our phone and email providers, who may use it only to provide that service to us.
  • With others when you give us written permission.
  • When the law requires it, or to prevent serious and immediate harm.

Text messages

If you give us your mobile number and agree to receive texts, we may text you about your inquiry, appointments and scheduling. We do not send marketing texts.

  • Consent to receive texts is not a condition of getting help from us.
  • Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply.
  • Reply STOP at any time to stop texts. Reply HELP for help, or call (636) 556-HOPE (4673).
  • No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging opt-in data and consent are never shared with any third party.

Text messages are not a secure way to send sensitive details. Please call us to talk about anything private.

Confidentiality

We treat what you share as confidential. We follow the federal and state confidentiality laws that apply to substance use treatment records, and we limit access to the people who need it to help you.

Your choices

You can ask us what information we have about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it where the law allows. You can stop texts by replying STOP and ask us to stop calling or emailing at any time.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will post the new version here with a new effective date.
